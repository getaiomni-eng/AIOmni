AIOmni
Fantasy manager • Live news • AI insights

AIOmni is an Expo/React Native app focused on fantasy football management — integrates leagues from Sleeper, ESPN, and Yahoo; fetches live news feeds; and generates AI-driven actionable insights for your roster.

What the app does
League Aggregation
Connect and view leagues from ESPN, Yahoo, and Sleeper.
AI Insights
Generates short, actionable recommendations per league using Anthropic APIs (configurable).
Live News Feed
Aggregates RSS headlines from Rotowire, PFR, and CBS Sports.
Team & Matchup Details
Shows scores, records, and matchup context with platform badges.
Onboarding & Auth
Supports OAuth flows (Yahoo), local onboarding, and settings sync.
Monetization
Includes paywall/payments and upsell banner components for subscriptions.
