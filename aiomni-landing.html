Why AIOmni

Every other app gives the same advice to every user.

Most fantasy tools assume half-PPR, 12-team, standard scoring. They publish one ranking and one start/sit answer for everyone. AIOmni reads your league's actual settings — every variable — before forming a single recommendation.

  • 4 vs 6
    Passing TDs completely change QB values. 6-point leagues push elite passers a full round earlier.
  • PPR
    Reception scoring redefines receiver tiers. Pass-catching backs shift from RB3 to RB1 territory.
  • TEP
    TE Premium turns Kelce, LaPorta, and Bowers into league-winners — and changes how you draft round 2.
  • SF
    SuperFlex makes any starting QB a top-30 asset. Your QB2 becomes gold.
League-aware by default

AIOmni inspects scoring rules, roster slots, waiver type, draft order, trade deadlines, and playoff structure on the first sync. Every ranking, trade grade, and start/sit answer is conditioned on those settings — not on an industry-default league nobody actually plays in.

How it works

Reads your league. Then advises.

Three steps. No setup overhead. Connect once, get personalized output across every tool.

Step 01

Connect your league

Link Sleeper, ESPN, Yahoo, MyFantasyLeague, or Fleaflicker. AIOmni reads your scoring format, roster construction, waiver type, and trade deadlines — every variable that makes your league unique.

Step 02

AI reads your settings

4-point vs 6-point passing TDs. Full PPR vs Standard. TE Premium. SuperFlex. Bonus thresholds. These completely change player values — AIOmni internalizes all of it before responding.

Step 03

Get personalized advice

Start/sit, waivers, trade grades, draft picks — every output is specific to your league's scoring and your actual roster. No generic top-100 list. No copy-pasted advice.

Features

Everything you need to draft, trade, and win — built on the same league-aware foundation.

AI Coach Pro

Conversational AI that already knows your roster, scoring rules, and league context. Ask anything — start/sit, waiver targets, trade strategy — and get an answer specific to your situation. Powered by Claude Opus.

Rankings

AIOmni Formula — a transparent, 4-stage algorithmic engine (VOR, age curves, opportunity adjustment, tier clustering) — plus Pulse, our blended community consensus across all five platforms.

Trade Analyzer Pro

A–F grades for both sides of every trade, calculated using your league's actual scoring settings and roster construction. Not generic surplus values — true context-aware grades.

The O · Draft Copilot Pro

Live draft assistance — real-time pick recommendations as your draft unfolds, factoring your exact scoring format, roster needs, opponent tendencies, and best-available value at every slot.

Season Memory Pro

AIOmni builds a profile of you as a manager — decisions, tendencies, outcomes — and remembers them across the full 52 weeks of the season. Every conversation gets sharper as the year progresses.

Live Data Feed

Injury reports, snap counts, Vegas lines, weather, advanced stats, college prospects, and beat-writer transactions — fed into every AI response with sources cited inline. Never advice without context.

Common questions

Quick answers

When does AIOmni launch?
Public launch is targeted for Summer 2026, ahead of the NFL season. Beta access is open now via the form — early users get extended free trials, direct line to the team, and grandfathered pricing.
Which fantasy platforms are supported?
Sleeper, ESPN, Yahoo, MyFantasyLeague, and Fleaflicker — all five included on every tier, including Free. We use official OAuth or public read endpoints where each platform provides them.
What makes AIOmni different from FantasyPros or DraftSharks?
Most tools publish one ranking and apply it to every user. AIOmni reads your league's exact scoring rules, roster construction, and waiver type before responding — so the same trade can be graded A for you and C for your league-mate. The algorithm (AIOmni Formula) is also documented in plain language, not a black box.
How are AI responses generated?
Coach-tier responses use Claude Opus, Anthropic's flagship reasoning model. Quick lookups use Claude Haiku for speed. Every prompt is enriched with your roster, league settings, current injury reports, snap counts, Vegas lines, and recent news before the model responds — sources are cited inline.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes. Monthly plans cancel any time with no fee. Annual plans are pro-rated. You keep access through the end of the billing period.
What's "Season Memory"?
A Pro feature that builds a profile of you as a manager — your tendencies, decisions, and outcomes — and remembers them across the full 52-week season. Each conversation becomes sharper as the year progresses because AIOmni knows what you've done before.
