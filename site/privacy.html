Privacy Policy

AIOmni ("AIOmni," "we," "us," or "our") is operated by AIOmni LLC, a Wyoming limited liability company. This Privacy Policy explains what information the AIOmni mobile app and website (the "Service") collect, how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices you have. By using the Service you agree to this Policy.

The short version: We collect only what's needed to run the app. We don't sell your data, don't track you across other apps or websites, and never use your conversations to train AI models. You can delete your account and data at any time.

1. Information we collect

We collect the following categories of data, all of it to provide the Service you ask for:

WhatWhy
Email addressAccount creation, authentication, password reset, and replying to support requests.
Account & user IDA unique account identifier (Supabase auth UUID) tied to the data you create, so we can sync your leagues and enforce your plan's prompt quota.
Fantasy-platform credentialsTo read your leagues, we store what each platform requires: your Sleeper username; ESPN session cookies (espn_s2/SWID); Yahoo OAuth tokens; and MFL / Fleaflicker league identifiers. OAuth tokens and ESPN cookies are stored in your device's secure keychain.
Your prompts & questionsThe text you type to the AI Coach and Trade Analyzer is processed to generate a response (see AI processing).
Uploaded screenshotsIf you upload a trade or draft-board screenshot, the image is sent to our AI provider to read the players in it, then used to generate your analysis. We do not store the image after processing.
Push tokenIf you enable notifications, we store your Expo/device push token to deliver player-news, lineup, and Pulse alerts.
Usage dataAggregate counts such as how many prompts you've used this week and when your leagues last synced — used to enforce quotas and measure feature engagement.
DiagnosticsCrash reports and performance data via Sentry, to find and fix bugs.

What we do NOT collect

We do not collect your location, health data, contacts, browsing history, or advertising identifiers (IDFA). We do not use any advertising SDKs.

2. How we use your information

  • To run the Service — authenticate you, connect and sync your leagues, generate AI advice conditioned on your league settings and roster, and deliver notifications you opt into.
  • To enforce plan limits — count AI prompts against your tier's weekly quota.
  • To support you — respond to emails and troubleshoot issues.
  • To improve the Service — diagnose crashes, monitor performance, and measure (in aggregate) which features are used.
  • To keep it secure — detect abuse, rate-limit, and prevent fraud.

3. AI processing

AIOmni's coaching features are powered by Anthropic's Claude models. When you ask a question or upload a screenshot, your input — enriched with your league settings, roster, and public data such as injury reports — is sent to Anthropic to generate a response. Your prompts and conversations are never used to train Claude or any other AI model, by us or by Anthropic. AI-generated output can be inaccurate; see our Terms of Service for important disclaimers about relying on it.

5. Payments

Subscriptions are sold through Apple's App Store and processed by Apple via StoreKit, with entitlements managed by RevenueCat. AIOmni never receives or stores your payment-card details — that information goes to Apple. We receive only the purchase/receipt data needed to unlock your plan.

6. Tracking and advertising

AIOmni does not track you. We do not link your data with data from other companies' apps or websites for advertising, we do not use the advertising identifier (IDFA), and we do not share data with advertising networks or data brokers. Because we don't track, the app does not show an App Tracking Transparency prompt.

7. How we protect your data

  • Data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest (AES-256 in Supabase).
  • Your fantasy-platform OAuth tokens and ESPN cookies are stored in your device's secure keychain, not in plain text.
  • AI requests run through an authenticated server-side proxy with rate limiting and abuse protection.

No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information using industry-standard safeguards.

8. Data retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account is active. You can delete your account at any time in the app via Settings → Account → Delete. When you do, your league connections, roster history, Season Memory, and AI conversation logs are permanently removed within 30 days (the window allows recovery from accidental deletion). To request immediate deletion, email aiomni@getaiomni.com.

9. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data, and to object to or restrict certain processing. California residents (CCPA/CPRA): you have the right to know what we collect, to delete it, and to not be discriminated against for exercising these rights — and note that we do not sell or "share" your personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. EEA/UK residents (GDPR): our legal bases are performance of our contract with you, your consent (e.g., notifications), and our legitimate interests in securing and improving the Service. To exercise any right, email aiomni@getaiomni.com and we'll respond as required by law.

10. Children

AIOmni is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Where your data is processed

AIOmni is operated from the United States, and our service providers may process and store data in the United States and other countries. By using the Service, you understand your information may be transferred to and processed in the U.S., where data-protection laws may differ from those in your country.

12. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy as the Service evolves. When we make material changes, we'll update the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, notify you in the app. Your continued use after changes take effect means you accept the revised Policy.

13. Contact us

Questions about this Policy or your data? Email aiomni@getaiomni.com or write to AIOmni LLC, Wyoming, USA.
