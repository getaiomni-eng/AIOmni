General

About AIOmni

When does AIOmni launch?
Public launch is targeted for Summer 2026, ahead of the NFL season. Beta access is open now — early users get free Pro through their first season, direct line to the team, and grandfathered pricing for life.
What makes AIOmni different from FantasyPros or DraftSharks?
Most tools publish one set of rankings and apply them to every user, assuming half-PPR scoring. AIOmni reads your league's exact scoring rules, roster construction, and waiver type before responding — so the same trade can be graded A for you and C for your league-mate. The algorithm (AIOmni Formula) is also documented in plain language, not a black box.
What does "league-aware" actually mean?
Every output is conditioned on your specific league settings. 4pt vs 6pt passing TDs change QB values by a full draft round. PPR vs Standard redefines receiver tiers. TE Premium pushes Trey McBride into the back half of round 2. SuperFlex makes any QB2 valuable. AIOmni internalizes all of this before generating advice — most tools just don't.
Platforms & data

Where we connect

Which fantasy platforms are supported?
Five platforms, included on every tier (even Free):
  • Sleeper — public read API (no auth needed for league data)
  • ESPN — session cookie auth for private leagues
  • Yahoo — official OAuth
  • MFL (MyFantasyLeague) — public ADP + cookie auth
  • Fleaflicker — cookie auth + public scraping for rankings
Where does live data come from?
Injuries from official NFL feeds. Snap counts from nflverse. Vegas implied team totals from the Odds API. Weather from OpenWeather (per stadium). College prospects from CFBD. News from Rotowire, PFR, CBS, ESPN, and beat-writer transactions. All sources are cited inline in every AI response.
Can I connect multiple leagues?
Yes. AIOmni handles unlimited leagues across all 5 platforms simultaneously. Each league keeps its own settings, roster, and Season Memory profile.
AI model

How the AI works

What AI model powers AIOmni?
Coach-tier responses use Claude Opus, Anthropic's flagship reasoning model. Quick player-card lookups use Claude Haiku for speed. Every prompt is enriched with your roster, league settings, current injury reports, snap counts, Vegas lines, and recent news before the model sees it — sources are cited inline in every answer.
What are "prompts" and why are they capped?
A prompt is one question to the AI Coach. Limits are tier-based (10 lifetime on Free, 25/week on Rankings, 50/week on Pro) to keep AI inference costs sustainable. Most active managers use 5–15 per week. Quick player-card buttons (e.g. "matchup", "news") do not count against your prompt budget.
Does AIOmni hallucinate?
Less than generic AI tools, but not zero. Every response is grounded in real data we inject before the prompt — injuries, snap counts, depth charts, news. Claude Opus is told explicitly to cite sources for any claim. If you spot an error, the in-app "report response" button helps us tune it.
Security & privacy

How we handle your data

How is my fantasy data stored?
League connections and roster data are encrypted at rest in Supabase (AES-256). Your OAuth tokens are encrypted in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest. We never sell, share, or use your data to train models.
Can I delete my account and data?
Yes — Settings → Account → Delete. All league connections, roster history, Season Memory, and AI conversation logs are permanently removed within 30 days (the window allows for accidental deletion recovery). Email aiomni@getaiomni.com if you want immediate deletion.
Do you use my data to train AI?
No. Your conversations with the AI Coach are never used as training data for Claude or any other model. Anthropic's API by default does not train on customer prompts, and we don't opt in.
