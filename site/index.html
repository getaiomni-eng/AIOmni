Why AIOmni

Every other app gives the same advice to every user.

Most fantasy tools assume half-PPR, 12-team, standard scoring. They publish one ranking and apply it to everyone. AIOmni reads your league's actual settings — every variable — before forming a single recommendation.

  • 4 vs 6
    Passing TDs change QB values by a full round. 6-point leagues push elite passers way up.
  • PPR
    Reception scoring redefines receiver tiers. Pass-catching backs shift from RB3 into RB1 territory.
  • TEP
    TE Premium turns McBride, Bowers, and LaPorta into league-winners.
  • SF
    SuperFlex makes any starter a top-30 asset. Your QB2 becomes gold.

League-aware by default

AIOmni inspects scoring rules, roster slots, waiver type, draft order, trade deadlines, and playoff structure on the first sync. Every ranking, trade grade, and start/sit answer is conditioned on those settings — not on an industry-default league nobody actually plays in.
