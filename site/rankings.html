Formula notes

Score is the raw 4-stage synthesis output — a relative VOR-derived value. Higher is better. Tier 1 is the elite cliff, Tier 5 is depth/dart-throws.

Dynasty formats apply the Time-Value Factor: a 26-year-old WR gets a multiplier boost vs a 31-year-old RB even if same VOR.

Pulse notes

Score is the blended weighted-average rank across all source platforms — lower is better. A score of 1.40 means roughly the #1.4 pick of the average draft.

Dynasty Pulse rows show KeepTradeCut trade value (higher = better) since dynasty community uses values, not ADP.
